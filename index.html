Abilities only fire when you discard the drawn card. Swapping it into your hand gives no ability.

How to Play Cabo

Objective

Have the lowest total hand value when "Cabo" is called. Kings = 0 pts, Aces = 1, J/Q = 10, all others face value.

Setup

Each player gets 4 face-down cards arranged in a 2×2 grid. At round start you secretly peek at your bottom 2 cards — memorise them!

On Your Turn

  1. Call Cabo — skip drawing; every other player takes one final turn, then hands are revealed
  2. Draw from Deck — flip the top deck card face-up
  3. Take Discard — take the top card from the discard pile

After drawing, pick one:

  • Swap with Hand — replace any of your 4 cards with the drawn card (your old card goes to discard). No ability fires.
  • Discard Drawn Card — put it in the discard pile. If it's 7/8/9/10/J/Q/K its special ability triggers!

Calling Cabo

Click Call Cabo at the start of your turn instead of drawing. All other players each get one final turn. Then all hands flip face-up.

⚠️ If you called Cabo but someone else has a lower (or equal) hand, you score your total + 10 penalty points.

Scoring & Winning

The round winner (lowest hand) scores their total. Everyone else scores their total. Reach 100 points → eliminated. Last player standing wins the game.
